# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Welcoming and inclusive language
- Respectful of differing viewpoints
- Graceful acceptance of constructive criticism

Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information.

Maintainers may remove contributions or ban contributors who violate this code.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
